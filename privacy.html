Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 6, 2026. This page explains the general information practices for The Roof Concierge website and its online estimate experience.

Information you choose to provide

You may provide contact details, a property address, roofing information, photos, measurements, or other details when using the instant estimator, calling, or emailing us. Only provide information that is relevant to your roofing request.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare or discuss estimates, arrange inspections or services, communicate about a roofing request, operate the website, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Website, advertising, and campaign information

Website hosting, security, analytics, or advertising measurement tools may process technical information such as device type, browser, pages visited, approximate location, referring page, and advertising campaign parameters. Campaign parameters may also be passed to the instant estimator so an inquiry can be connected with the source that referred it.

Third-party services

The website uses an embedded estimator provided by Roofr. Information entered into that estimator may be processed by Roofr and is subject to its applicable terms and privacy practices. Hosting, email, security, and advertising providers may also process information as needed to provide their services.

Cookies and consent choices

Analytics or advertising tools may use cookies or similar technologies when enabled. Where consent is required, the website should provide a way to communicate that choice before applicable advertising or analytics storage is used.

Your choices

You may choose not to use the online estimator and contact us by phone or email instead. For questions about information connected with a roofing inquiry, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for property owners and business customers and is not directed to children under 13.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website, estimate process, advertising tools, or business practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
